I’m an Electrical Engineer working on the systems behind next-generation wireless networks. I focus on turning 5G/6G research into integrated, testable environments, with a particular interest in network security, resilience and the practical role of AI.

I care about how networks behave beyond the diagram: how components fit together, how failures surface, and how an experiment becomes repeatable and useful. My approach is hands-on, systems-minded and grounded in making complex setups work end to end.
